# Site ka risk check aur facilities ki behtar jagah

Date: 2026-10-16
Status: Idea recorded; advanced features pending

## Idea kya tha?

User boundary kisi bhi jagah bana sakta hai. Design se pehle software us jagah ki available information dekh kar bataye ke wahan kya mushkil ho sakti hai. Design banne ke baad school, hospital aur doosri facilities ke liye behtar jagah suggest kare.

## Boundary banne par kya dekhna hai?

- Zameen seedhi hai ya dhalan par hai?
- Pani, lake, river ya samandar kahan hai?
- Flood, landslide aur doosre hazards ka available record kya kehta hai?
- Existing road tak asal rasta hai ya sirf map par paas nazar aa rahi hai?
- Land use ya protected area ki koi restriction available hai?
- Har result ka source, date aur accuracy kya hai?

Sirf satellite image ya map ka rang dekh kar final faisla nahi karna. Data na mile to "Not checked" ya "Unknown" dikhana hai. Sirf pani ke qareeb hona flood ka proof nahi, aur purana flood record na milna safety ka proof nahi.

## Site ke andar kya suggest karna hai?

- School tak bachon ka safe walking route aur distance.
- Hospital tak roads ke zariye pohanchne ka waqt.
- Park tak har residential block ki access.
- Mosque, church aur community centre ke liye jagah aur parking.
- Commercial area ke traffic, parking aur nearby homes par asar.

## Example

Hospital map ke beech mein rakhne se zaroori nahi sab ko achhi access mile. Software road network, entrances aur homes ki location dekh kar 2 ya 3 options compare kare. Har option ke saath wajah likhe.

## Screen par kaise dikhana hai?

- Green: available checks mein koi issue nahi mila; final approval nahi.
- Yellow: review ki zaroorat.
- Red: verified conflict ya selected rule ke mutabiq avoid area.
- Grey: zaroori data nahi mila.

## Kya baqi hai?

Basic site analysis aur concept checks project mein hain. Reliable NZ terrain/hazard data, source freshness, road travel-time analysis aur ranked facility suggestions abhi alag kaam hain. Inhen complete samajh kar checklist tick nahi karni.
