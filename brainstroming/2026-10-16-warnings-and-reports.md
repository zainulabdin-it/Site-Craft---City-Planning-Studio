# Inspector ke saath Warnings tab

Date: 2026-10-16
Status: Implemented

## Masla kya tha?

Inspector kholne par building overlap ki warning screen ke upar phail jati thi. Map aur controls chhup jate thay. Report aur object edit karne ki cheezen bhi aik jagah thi.

## Idea kya tha?

Right side par Inspector ke saath alag Warnings tab ho. Is mein current project ke checks, warning details aur Planning report ka button ho.

## Ab kya hota hai?

- Band panel ke right bar se Inspector ya Warnings seedha khol sakte hain.
- Warnings ki ginti button par nazar aati hai.
- Puri warning list dikhti hai; sirf pehli 6 warnings nahi.
- Object wali warning par click karen to us object ka Inspector khul jata hai aur map us par jata hai.
- Issue fix karne par list dobara calculate hoti hai aur warning khud hat jati hai.
- Undo se issue wapas aaye to warning bhi wapas aa jati hai.
- Planning report isi tab se khulti hai.

## Example

Do ghar aik doosre ke upar aa rahe hain. Warnings mein "Buildings overlap" aayega. Aik ghar ko sahi jagah move karne ya delete karne par yeh warning khatam ho jayegi.

Yeh current issues ki list hai. Purani resolved warnings ka permanent history log abhi nahi banaya gaya. Data available na ho to us ka check unknown rahega.
